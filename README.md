# ⚖️ Political Compass Test – Global Compass 2026

A modern, responsive, client-side web application for assessing political viewpoints based on a 2-dimensional model (Political Compass 2D). Features **140 balanced questions**, **44 political ideologies**, **105 world leaders & historical figures**, and **30 international party families**.

Available in 6 languages: **English (EN - default)**, **Polish (PL)**, **Spanish (ES)**, **German (DE)**, **Russian (RU)**, and **French (FR)**.

---

## 🎯 Key Features & Capabilities

### 1. 🌐 Multilingual Architecture (6 Languages with English Default)
- Language selection available directly in the header and setup modal:
  - 🇬🇧 **English** (`en` - Default)
  - 🇵🇱 **Polish** (`pl`)
  - 🇪🇸 **Spanish** (`es`)
  - 🇩🇪 **German** (`de`)
  - 🇷🇺 **Russian** (`ru`)
  - 🇫🇷 **French** (`fr`)
- 100% translation parity across all 140 questions, 12 categories, 44 ideologies, 105 politician profiles, 30 party family manifestos, 6 answer choices, and comprehensive UI strings.
- Dynamic recalculation of canvas axis labels and quadrant descriptions upon language switching.

### 2. 📋 140 Balanced Questions Across 12 Key Domains
- **Perfect balance**: 70 economic (`econ`) questions and 70 social/cultural (`soc`) questions.
- **Strict polarity balance**: Exactly 35 positive (`+1`) and 35 negative (`-1`) questions on each axis (5 positive and 5 negative questions across each of the 12 categories).
- **Two Test Modes**:
  - ⚡ **Quick Mode (30 questions)**: ~3-5 minutes, representative balanced sample covering all 12 domains.
  - 🧭 **Full Mode (140 questions)**: ~12-15 minutes, in-depth political audit.
- **12 Comprehensive Categories**:
  1. **Economy & Free Market** (deregulation, monopoly prevention, state interventionism)
  2. **Taxation & Public Finance** (flat vs progressive tax, wealth tax, fiscal incentives)
  3. **Labor, Wages & Unions** (minimum wage, working hours, labor contracts, automation tax)
  4. **Social Welfare & Housing** (public pensions, UBI, social housing, welfare conditionality)
  5. **Privatization & Industry Regulation** (rail, utilities, professional licensing, strategic banking)
  6. **Global Trade & Corporations** (free trade vs protectionism, tariffs, Big Tech regulation)
  7. **Civil Liberties & Privacy** (free speech, gun rights, euthanasia, surveillance)
  8. **Technology, AI & Digital Rights** (cryptocurrency/DeFi, E2E encryption, AI licensing, CBDCs)
  9. **Climate, Ecology & Energy** (carbon neutrality, fossil bans, nuclear energy, animal welfare)
  10. **Culture, Tradition & Religion** (state-church separation, marriage equality, secularism, cultural identity)
  11. **Human Rights & Social Issues** (gender parity, refugee asylum, justice reform, social equality)
  12. **Security, Borders & Geopolitics** (defense alliances, border security, international treaties, deterrence)

- **Interactive Category Filtering**: Switch politician rankings between Top 12, All 105, Contemporary, World War II, History, and Activists. Switch party rankings between Top 8, All 30, Left, Market, Libertarian, and Conservative.
- **12 Sector Breakdown**: Individual percentage score meters for each policy domain.
- **High-DPI PNG Card Export**: Generate and download a sleek, shareable summary card (1080x1280 PNG) optimized for social media sharing.
- **Clipboard Summary Copy**: One-click text summary export with clipboard fallback support.

- **Keyboard Navigation**: Keys `1`-`5` for fast answers, arrow keys for navigation.
- **100% Privacy & Zero Tracking**: Fully client-side execution, no analytics, no third-party trackers, no cookies.

## 🚀 Quick Start

The application is completely static and requires no build tools or servers to run:

1. Clone the repository:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/<your-username>/test-polityczny.git
   cd test-polityczny
   ```
2. Open **`index.html`** in any modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, Opera, Brave).
